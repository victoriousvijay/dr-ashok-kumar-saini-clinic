# MVP Plan

## Objective

Create a client-demo-ready product that can be shown to Dr. Ashok Kumar Saini as a working business system rather than a design concept.

## MVP Scope

### Public Website
- Premium 3D hero
- Services
- Doctor profile
- Clinic information
- Appointment CTA
- Contact/location
- Service SEO pages
- Responsive design
- SEO metadata
- Sitemap/robots
- Conversion forms

### CRM
- Login
- Dashboard
- Leads
- Lead pipeline
- Patient profiles
- Appointments
- Follow-ups
- Notes
- Search/filter
- Basic metrics
- Role-aware access

## Dashboard KPIs
Show:
- New leads
- Leads awaiting contact
- Today's appointments
- Upcoming appointments
- Follow-ups due
- Conversion rate
- Lead sources

## Demo Data
Use clearly marked synthetic demo records only.
Never use real patient information in development or demos.

Example:
- Rahul Sharma — Anxiety & Stress — Website
- Neha Verma — Child & Adolescent Psychiatry — Google
- Amit Singh — Sleep Clinic — WhatsApp

## Website Experience

### Hero
Headline direction:
"Specialist Mental Health Care, Built Around You."

Supporting copy:
"Psychiatric consultation, psychotherapy and mental health services in Jaipur with a patient-first approach."

Primary CTA:
"Book an Appointment"

Secondary CTA:
"Explore Services"

Do not publish this copy as final until approved by the doctor.

### Visual Direction
Use a dark premium hero with:
- Soft 3D depth
- Floating translucent forms
- Slow ambient movement
- Subtle medical-inspired geometry
- Strong readable typography
- Clean CTA hierarchy

Then transition into light/neutral content sections for trust and readability.

## CRM UX

### Leads
Columns:
- Name
- Phone
- Interested service
- Source
- Status
- Assigned to
- Created
- Next action

Lead drawer:
- Contact information
- Enquiry
- Timeline
- Status
- Assign staff
- Add note
- Schedule appointment
- Add follow-up

### Patients
Profile:
- Basic information
- Appointment history
- Follow-ups
- Notes
- Activity timeline

Keep sensitive data minimal and protected.

## Definition of Done

The MVP is done when:
- A visitor can understand the clinic in under 30 seconds.
- A visitor can find a relevant service page.
- A visitor can submit an enquiry.
- The enquiry appears in CRM.
- Staff can move the lead through the pipeline.
- Staff can create an appointment.
- Staff can schedule a follow-up.
- The website is mobile responsive.
- The site has proper local SEO foundations.
- CRM private data is protected by authentication + RLS.
